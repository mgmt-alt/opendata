The SkillCorner Score — a FIFA-style rating

One number per player, 0–100, built from position-relative silos — each a weighted blend of metrics ranked as percentiles within the player's position, combined weighted by position by default (a striker leans on Creation & Movement, a centre-back on Physical & Passing) or with your own weights. All eight silos are always in play — the same set as the radar below. Pace/Physical/Passing/Creation/Movement come from the full-season aggregates; Shooting · Defending · Dribbling come from the 10 tracked matches, so the ranking covers the players who feature in them.

Scope: Pace/Physical/Passing/Creation/Movement are full-season aggregates (percentiled within position); Shooting, Defending and Dribbling are from the 10 tracked matches (volume over the sample, percentiled league-wide). The ranking therefore covers players who feature in those matches. No xG exists in the open data.
Eligibility: 3+ matches (use the “Min matches” filter above) and data in all eight silos. Click any row to load that player's profile below.

Shooting & finishing 10-match sample

The season aggregates have no shots — but the per-match dynamic events do (a possession ending in a shot). This covers only the 10 tracked matches (~38% of the roster) and has no xG, so it's shot volume & goals, kept separate from the season score above. Filtered by position.

“Apps” = tracked matches the player featured in. Goals = shots whose possession led to a goal.

The athletic map

Passers: volume, security & risk

Pass volume against completion rate. Colour shows whether a player completes more than expected given pass difficulty (blue) or fewer (red) — SkillCorner's xPass model sets the bar.

Scatter explorer — find your own nuggets

The open sandbox: plot any metric against any other, then size and colour the dots by a third and fourth. Every metric that feeds the silos — plus dozens of extra season and 10-match measures — is on the menu. Filtered by position above; hover a dot for detail, click it to open that player's card.

Player card & profile

The eight silo faces (like a FIFA card) plus the percentile detail behind them. Each bar's centre line is the median (of positional peers, or of all teams in Team view); right (blue) is above average, left (red) below. Use the View toggle up top to switch between a player card and a team card.
