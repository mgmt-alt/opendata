The SkillCorner Score — a FIFA-style rating

One number per player, 0–100, built from five silos the tracking data can actually see. Each silo is a weighted blend of metrics, each ranked as a percentile within the player's position. The overall combines the silos — weighted by position by default (a striker leans on Creation & Movement, a centre-back on Physical & Passing), or set your own weights.

What tracking can't see: this is broadcast tracking data (movement + on-ball passing), so three FIFA faces are not measurable and are deliberately left out — Shooting (no shots/xG per player), Dribbling (no 1v1 events) and Defending (no tackles/interceptions/duels).
Eligibility: 3+ matches (use the “Min matches” filter above) and data in all five silos. Percentiles use the full positional pool, so a player's standing never depends on the filter. Click any row to load that player's profile below.
